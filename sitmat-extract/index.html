How to read this
  • Period. The extract covers about six months: one dry-season drawdown and the start of the wet season. It cannot separate a long-term trend from seasonal drawdown, so read the numbers as drawdown since April and recession rate.
  • Panel. Only stations with data in at least the chosen share of weeks, and a baseline in their first weeks, enter change and trend statistics.
  • Significance. The Mann–Kendall test ignores autocorrelation in weekly series, so “significant” is indicative only.
  • Rain and soil moisture. The portal returns 0 for every concession reading, so they are not used. A water balance (neraca air) needs an external rainfall source such as CHIRPS or GPM IMERG.
  • Community stations have low coverage and inconsistent values, so they are opt-in via the Source filter.

Water-table duration curve: early vs late

How much of the time the water table sits at or above each depth, panel stations, first third of the period against the last third. A curve that moves left and down means more days spent deep.

Longest dry spell per station

Longest run of consecutive days below −40 cm (gaps of up to 2 days do not break a run). Runs still open at the end of the period are counted as they stand, so they are lower bounds.

Breadth: how many stations rise vs fall each day

Share of stations rising by more than 1 cm minus the share falling by more than 1 cm, using only stations with readings on two consecutive days. Synchronised blue bars are wetting pulses (rain events), red runs are drying spells. No rainfall data is needed, and membership changes cannot create the signal.

Breadth by province and week

Weekly mean of the daily breadth. Rows that light up together point to a region-wide weather signal; a lone row points to something local.

Duration curves by province

Each panel is a province (blue) against all panel stations (grey). Dashed lines mark −40 and −80 cm; the figure is the share of station-days below −40 cm.

Time below −40 cm by concession

Mean over each concession's stations of the share of days below −40 cm (dot), with the spread of its stations (10th to 90th percentile, bar). Concessions are identified by the portal's company code. Few stations make noisy estimates, and this describes readings, not compliance.
