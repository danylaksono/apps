How the badges are computed

Local scale factors are measured from the projection itself by finite differences on a 12° grid, then combined into the Tissot semi-axes. Area spread is the ratio between the 98th and 2nd percentile of local area scale; shape is the median and 98th-percentile angular distortion. A projection is labelled equal-area or conformal only when the measurements say so.
